## Design Context

### Users

macOS developers and knowledge workers who dictate into whichever app currently
has focus. They need fast, glanceable feedback while keeping their attention in
Cursor, an editor, a browser, email, chat, documents, or another productivity
app.

### Brand Personality

Fast, calm, trustworthy.

### Aesthetic Direction

A compact, dark OLED voice HUD inspired by the supplied Gemini Live / Typeless
reference: a compact 132×34 pill, restrained contrast, clear state changes, and
quiet system-level presence. The settings surface should be practical and
focused rather than decorative. Blur, large glow stacks, and motion that costs
responsiveness are deliberately avoided on the always-on-top transparent panel.

### Design Principles

1. State feedback must be immediate and legible without stealing focus.
2. Context adaptation should be visible but conservative: preserve the user's
   intent when confidence is low.
3. Motion communicates recording, processing, completion, and failure; it never
   competes with the active application.
4. Privacy and target safety are product-facing behaviors, not hidden plumbing.
5. Every fallback keeps the user's text recoverable.
